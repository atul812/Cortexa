Cortexa — Live Call Transcription

How to use:

  1. Open a video call (Google Meet, Zoom, etc.)
  2. A floating panel will appear on the page
  3. Click "Start" to begin transcription
  4. Allow microphone access when prompted
  5. Speak into your microphone - text will appear in real-time

Important tips:

  • Use headphones to avoid echo/feedback
  • Works best in Chrome or Edge browser
  • Transcribes your microphone only (your side of the conversation)
  • All processing happens in your browser - no audio is uploaded
This extension uses your microphone only. It does not upload audio anywhere.
